# Research

- When reading PDFs, inspect both the text layer and rendered page images.
  Use OCR for image-only or image-heavy pages, including tables, charts,
  footnotes, and risk disclosures. Reconcile material figures and disclosures
  against the extracted text; if OCR or visual inspection is unavailable or
  uncertain, record the limitation and do not treat the affected claim as
  verified.
