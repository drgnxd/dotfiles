# Research Workflow

- Define the question, decision, scope, and freshness requirements before
  collecting sources.
- Prefer primary sources where practical. Triangulate material claims and seek
  conflicting or disconfirming evidence.
- When reading PDFs, inspect both the text layer and rendered page images.
  Use OCR for image-only or image-heavy pages, including tables, charts,
  footnotes, and risk disclosures. Reconcile material figures and disclosures
  against the extracted text; if OCR or visual inspection is unavailable or
  uncertain, record the limitation and do not treat the affected claim as
  verified.
- Distinguish established facts, inference, speculation, and unknowns.
- Record source provenance and limitations when they materially affect the
  conclusion.
- Stop when additional sources no longer change the material conclusion.
