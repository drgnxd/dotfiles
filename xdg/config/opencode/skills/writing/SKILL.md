# Writing

Start from audience, purpose, deliverable and required tone. Outline before drafting only if the artifact is substantial. Keep claims supported and distinguish user-supplied facts from outside evidence. Prioritize clarity, accuracy, and natural flow. Verify completeness and requested formatting. Do not add fabricated citations, quotes or statistics.
