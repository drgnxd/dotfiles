# Research Design

1. State the decision or question the research serves and its date sensitivity.
2. Separate known evidence, hypotheses and assumptions. Identify the fewest independent questions needed.
3. Prefer primary sources (official documents, standards, filings, raw datasets) and independently corroborate material claims.
4. Define comparison dimensions and a stopping rule before collecting more sources.
5. For multi-source research, attribute every material claim to a source URL and publication or as-of date; explicitly flag uncertainty and single-source or unsourced claims, preserve conflicting evidence without averaging, and mark sources over 12 months old. Never treat search snippets as definitive evidence.
6. Use the main agent for trivial scope setting; call a strategist (Claude Code: `Plan`) only for meaningful ambiguity or trade-offs.
