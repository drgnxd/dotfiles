# Decision Analysis

Define the actual objective and non-negotiable constraints. Identify the viable alternatives, including doing nothing. Compare them on explicit criteria, costs, reversibility, uncertainty and second-order effects. Distinguish factual evidence from judgments and preferences. Prefer a decision with a review trigger rather than false precision. Summarize the preferred option, strongest objection and conditions under which the recommendation changes.
